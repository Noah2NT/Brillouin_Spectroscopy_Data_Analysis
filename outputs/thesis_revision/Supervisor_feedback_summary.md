### Advice Noah reported from the in-person discussion

- Build paragraphs around a leading idea, followed by the relevant explanation and evidence. Avoid isolated single-sentence paragraphs and do not merge unrelated subjects just to make paragraphs longer.
- Stop burying the lead. In particular, the difficulty of measuring mechanical properties belongs near the start of the introduction.
- Reduce the number of subsections where they interrupt the flow; this was a secondary concern rather than a demand to remove every subdivision.
- Introduce 0–10% glycerol by mass as the initial range and state that it may change if pilot results are not useful.
- Add references for strong or general factual claims, or qualify/remove the claims.
- Replace concentration symbol c because it can be confused with the speed of light. The revision uses w_g, with maximum w_max; acoustic speed is v_L.

## Additional technical revisions made while implementing the advice

These are editorial or modelling decisions from this revision, not extra statements attributed to either supervisor.

- Used ordinary-frequency BFS notation f_B consistently, avoiding a possible angular-frequency interpretation of Omega.
- Distinguished intrinsic linewidth from instrument-broadened linewidth and peak area from peak height.
- Retained the supplied provisional concentration coefficients, but removed any implication that they are a fitted 660 nm calibration.
- Replaced a purely continuous concentration density with a probability measure that can include an atom at pure water.
- Defined collection-weighted fractions separately from signal fractions, especially when area changes with concentration.
- Noted that the long collection interval supplies no pure sample ray in the baseline. This directly affects extreme-observation PCA methods and VCA.
- Reformulated the fourth configuration in BFS and signal-contribution coordinates. An unknown intensity function and unknown material amount can compensate each other; one or two reference spectra do not in general resolve that ambiguity.
- Supplied proposed numerical conditions, photon budgets, independent realisations, tuning rules, convergence checks and practical recovery tolerances. These choices are proposals to discuss/freeze after pilot work, not supervisor-approved settings or completed experiments.
- Generated concentration-parameter and noiseless-dataset figures, with captions explicitly stating what is modelled.
- Kept the original illustration filenames as optional placeholders and revised their captions/briefs. A missing image displays a placeholder box instead of preventing compilation.
