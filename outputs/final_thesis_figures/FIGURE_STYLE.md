# Proposed thesis illustration style

Status: approved by Noah. Option A (Clean journal) is locked in for all report figures.

Noah liked the palette and intracellular details in option C, but explicitly selected A. Use A as the consistent style; retain the C preference as context for any later requested refinements.

- Font: Arial throughout. Mathematical symbols use matching Arial styling.
- Background: white; no gradients, decorative shadows or three-dimensional effects.
- Illustration: original flat vector schematics, with equipment and cell anatomy simplified to what explains the measurement.
- Main text/outlines: dark grey #263238. Secondary guides: #7A858B.
- Comparison palette: blue #24649A and orange #BE5A26. These are comparison colours, not universal biological labels; their meaning will be stated in each figure.
- Curves: use both colour and line style when comparing quantities; use consistent axes and limits where comparison requires them.
- Cell schematic: pale blue-grey fill #E7EFF2, muted nucleus #BCCDD5 and outline #607C86.
- Labels: sentence case, short direct labels, bold panel headings and lettered panels.
- Graphs: retain physical units for quantitative plots. Schematic plots omit numerical ticks and are identified as illustrative in the caption.
- Export: PDF for Overleaf, editable SVG, and 300 dpi PNG preview. Provide separate caption text.
- Approval workflow: candidates live in drafts; only agreed versions move into approved. Assemble the final export folder and archive after the figure-by-figure review.
